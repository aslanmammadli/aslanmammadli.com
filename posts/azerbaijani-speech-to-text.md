Speech recognition works well for English because there is an enormous amount of English audio to train on. Azerbaijani does not have that luxury. To build a good model, I first had to build the dataset.

## Collecting the data

I ran a two month crowdsourcing competition to collect Azerbaijani speech. **212 participants** contributed recordings, and by the end we had **750 hours** of speech to train on.

A competition turned data collection from a chore into something people wanted to take part in, and it brought in a wide range of voices instead of a handful of speakers.

## Where it is used

The resulting speech to text model is used by **ASAN**, the State Agency for Public Service and Social Innovations under the President of Azerbaijan, for customer service.

## Why it matters

Languages with less available data are easy for large tech companies to deprioritize. This project showed that a focused effort with a motivated community can close a lot of that gap.
