CEO and Co-founder, Enverson

I build AI products that help people learn languages. I am the CEO and co-founder of Enverson, a voice first AI English tutoring platform that lets learners practice real conversations out loud.

Before Enverson, I spent six years running physical English language schools with my co-founder Emil Mammadov, and I co-founded Enverson Colleges, an education group with campuses in Baku, Sumgait and London. We pivoted the company to AI and launched the product in January 2025.
