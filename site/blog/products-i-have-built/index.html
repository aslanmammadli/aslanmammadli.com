The products I have built so far

November 3, 2026 · Aslan Mammadli

A quick map of what I have built, as a starting point for this blog.

Buqelemun

A consumer app that grew to 60,000 monthly active users.

Enverson Colleges

An education group with campuses in Baku, Sumgait and London, which I co-founded after years of running English language schools.

Azerbaijani speech to text

A speech recognition model trained on 750 hours of Azerbaijani speech, collected through a crowdsourcing competition with 212 participants. It is used by ASAN for customer service.

Enverson AI

My current company: a voice first AI English tutor that lets learners practice real conversations out loud. I am the CEO and co-founder.

Along the way I have also trained more than 3,000 people in entrepreneurship. I will write about lessons from each of these here.
